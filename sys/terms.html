Purpose

Sys is a personal, non-commercial Windows desktop project for reading and organizing information from services explicitly authorized by its user. The public website is informational and does not provide sign-in or a hosted Sys account.

Account authorization

Users must have lawful access to any connected academic or Google account and must comply with applicable institutional policies. Each Google account is separately authorized through Google's own OAuth interface. Access from an institutional Workspace account may be subject to administrative controls and restrictions.

Read-only operation

Sys is designed to retrieve and index information; its current integrations do not send or modify Gmail messages or update academic records. Calendar and Drive support are planned and are not yet available. Source platforms remain authoritative for email and academic information.

Local responsibility

Users are responsible for protecting their device, local SQLite database, backups and Windows account. Permissions may be revoked in Google Account settings. Revocation does not automatically remove copies already stored locally; see the privacy policy.
